# OkCredit annual reports — 2020 and 2021

While I was a Senior Business Analyst at OkCredit (Jul 2020 – Dec 2021), I created OkCredit's public year-end reports. They combine OkCredit's proprietary transaction data from small merchants across India with public economic data.

| Report | Pages | |
|---|---:|---|
| **2020 — *All's well that ends well*.** A look at what was bought, and how, at small and medium businesses across India. | 17 | [PDF](OkCredit_Annual_Report_2020.pdf) |
| **2021 — *Decoding Informal BNPL*.** A study of trust-based credit at mom-and-pop stores: credit size, credit cycles, NPAs, payments, user segments and network effects. | 49 | [PDF](OkCredit_Annual_Report_2021.pdf) |

**What I did:** framed the questions, pulled and analysed the data, built the insights and storyline, and worked with design to produce the final reports.
